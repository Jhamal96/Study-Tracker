Welcome to Study Tracker ♡

A gentle study-and-break timer to help you focus without forgetting to rest.

  • 📚
    Choose your study timeSet how many minutes you want to study, and tap Start when you're ready.
  • ☕
    Enjoy a little breakSet a separate break timer. The app alternates between study and rest; turn on auto-start for hands-free sessions.
  • ✨
    Celebrate your little winsChoose a round goal, see how many study sessions you finish, and enjoy a gentle chime when it's time to switch.
  • 🪟
    Keep the timer nearbyTry the floating timer button while studying in another window. Picture-in-Picture support depends on your browser.
  • 💗
    A little extra encouragementEnjoy uplifting motivational and Gospel quotes on your home page. Your settings save here automatically, with optional cloud sync.
